# Name products for the outcome, never for somebody else's repository

The agents this offer starts from exist as public templates, and two of them carry the titles "Software factory" and "Marketing team". Those titles are the obvious names to sell under, and a reader recognises them at once. We name our products for the outcome they deliver instead. The first one is "Issue to pull request".

## Considered options

Taking the template titles costs nothing on the day and breaks two rules we already keep. The Claims section of `README.md` forbids carrying a name out of those repositories into our own product line, and `CONTEXT.md` reserves "software factory on demand" for the company promise, so "Software Factory" as a product name collides with our own headline.

Coining brand names reads well and teaches a stranger nothing. A visitor who wants their issues turned into pull requests cannot tell what "Atlas" does until they have read a paragraph, and the price sits beside the name.

## Consequences

An outcome name states what the money buys and where the agent stops, which is the work the authority line also does. "Issue to pull request" says the agent opens a draft and a named person signs the merge.

The names give up the search traffic that "software factory" and "AI marketing team" carry. Copy inside a product page uses those phrases descriptively, where they belong to the job rather than to us.

A standing agent still answers to whatever name the client's team addresses it by, which ADR 0002 fixed and this decision leaves alone. The product name is ours. The agent's name is the client's.
