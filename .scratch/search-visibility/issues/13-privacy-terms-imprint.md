# The site states how it handles data

Status: ready-for-agent
Source: GitHub issue #20, opened on 2026-09-20.

## What to build

The site publishes no privacy policy, no terms and no imprint. It is an EU company, it collects an email address through every call to action, and it is about to start measuring visits. A buyer of a ten thousand euro build looks for these pages before a first call, and a search engine reads their absence as a trust signal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A privacy page names the analytics processor chosen, the mailbox, what is collected, the lawful basis, and how a visitor asks for deletion.
- [ ] Terms of service are published, stating what a build buys and what the monthly work covers, consistent with the prices the site prints.
- [ ] The imprint details an EU buyer expects are published: legal name, registered address, registration number, and VAT number where one applies.
- [ ] The footer links every one of them.
- [ ] Each page is in the sitemap with its copy date, and carries its canonical URL and graph nodes.
